This is a complete walkthrough for Sunspire Saga, the browser-based pixel-art
RPG. It covers every area from the first step in Thera to the top of the
Sunspire, every treasure chest, all eight spirits (the game's "djinn"), both
bosses, the shops, the bestiary and the mechanics behind the numbers.

Everything in this document comes straight out of the game's own data file
(game.js), so the numbers are exact for the version listed above. If the game
has moved on since then, check the changelog page and section [VER02].

===============================================================================
  1. INTRODUCTION                                                      [INT01]
===============================================================================

Sunspire Saga is an original homage to the handheld JRPGs of the early 2000s:
a party of four elemental adepts, eight collectible spirits that boost your
stats when "set" and power great summons when "unleashed", turn-based battles
with hidden enemy HP, towns, shops, an inn, treasure chests and a dark tower
at the end of the road.

The whole game runs in the browser from one script with no install. It saves
in your browser's local storage, so play it from the same browser (and the
same site address) if you want to continue a game.

The game is short - a focused adventure of a couple of hours - but it is not a
pushover. From version 31 on, enemies bite, spirits and bosses shrug off plain
sword swings, and the four late spirits act twice a turn. This guide tells you
where everything is and how to get through the hard parts without grinding
more than you need to.

How to use this guide: section 7 is the walkthrough in the order the game
expects you to play it, with a map, an item list and the enemies for every
area. Sections 8 to 13 are reference lists if you only want to look something
up. Map coordinates are written as (x,y): x counts tiles from the left edge,
y counts tiles from the top, both starting at 0, and the rulers on the maps
show them.

===============================================================================
  3. STORY                                                             [STO03]
===============================================================================

The Sunspire, the great tower north of the village of Thera, has gone dark.
With its light gone, monsters prowl every road and the folk of Thera and the
mining town of Brindle have bolted their doors. The eight elemental spirits
that once lived in the spire scattered across the land when the SHADOW WYRM
took the top of the tower.

Ari, a young earth adept from Thera, is sent out by the elder Sorin to gather
the spirits and bring the dawn back. Along the way Ari is joined by Lyn, a
fire adept scouting ahead in Brindle; Bram, a water adept and miner who knows
every shaft of Brindle Mine; and Sera, a wind adept pinned down by harpies
on the Sunspire Trail.

===============================================================================
  4. CONTROLS AND THE MENU                                             [CTL04]
===============================================================================

Keyboard
--------
   Arrow keys / WASD   Move (overworld), move the cursor (menus)
   Z or Space          A button: confirm, talk, open chests, read signs
   X or Esc            B button: cancel, back, close the menu
   Enter               START: open / close the pause menu; also confirms
                       on the title screen and skips battle messages
   M                   Toggle sound
   F                   Toggle immersive (fullscreen) mode

Touch / mouse
-------------
The handheld shell around the screen has a clickable D-pad, A, B and START
buttons.

Interacting
-----------
Face a thing and press A: villagers, signs, doors, chests, the boulder and
the spire gate all respond to A. Spirits are different - you walk onto them
and the duel starts at once (see [BAS05], 5.4). Bosses start their fight the
moment you try to step into them, or when you face them and press A.

The pause menu (START / Enter)
------------------------------
   STATUS   One member at a time (left/right to switch): level, XP to next
            level, HP, PP, ATK and DEF (total, with the base in brackets),
            AGI, weapon, armour and the arts learned so far.
   ITEMS    Use a Herb, Potion, Psy Drop or Elixir on a member. Lyn's
            Compass shows here as a KEY item while you carry it.
   EQUIP    Left/right picks a member, up/down picks the weapon or armour
            slot, A opens the list of gear you own. "REMOVE" unequips.
            Gear is shared: unequipping puts it back in the pool.
   SPIRITS  The eight spirits, named once found, "?????" until then.
   SAVE     Writes the save to the browser. A toast confirms it.

The main menu page also shows your coins, spirits found (x/8), the party
level, every member's HP and PP bars, and a QUEST line that always tells you
the next story step. If you are ever lost, open the menu and read it.

===============================================================================
  5. GAME BASICS                                                       [BAS05]
===============================================================================

5.1 Party and levels
--------------------
The whole party shares ONE level. Experience from a battle goes to the party,
and when the bar fills everyone levels up at once, all HP and PP are refilled,
and anyone whose art is due at that level learns it. New members join at the
party's current level, so nobody is ever behind.

XP needed to reach the next level is 18 + 12 x (current level):

   Level   XP for next   Total XP from level 1
     1         30              0
     2         42             30
     3         54             72
     4         66            126
     5         78            192
     6         90            270
     7        102            360
     8        114            462
     9        126            576
    10        138            702
    11        150            840

Each level adds a fixed amount to every stat; see [CHR06] for the growth of
each character.

5.2 Battle commands
-------------------
Commands are picked from a row of icons at the bottom-left, one member at a
time in party order. Left/right (or up/down) moves between icons, A picks, B
goes back to the previous member's command. Once everyone has a command the
round plays out in AGI order (plus a little randomness). Enemy HP is hidden;
an enemy's name shows only while you are targeting it.

   FIGHT    A plain weapon attack on one enemy. Damage is your ATK (base +
            weapon + 1 per set spirit) + 0 to 4, minus the enemy's DEF.
            Spirits in their duel form take only 30% of this, bosses 50%
            (Ogre) or 45% (Wyrm). Against those, FIGHT is a last resort.
   ARTS     Elemental attacks and healing. Costs PP. See 5.3.
   SPIRIT   Unleash a set spirit. See 5.4.
   SUMMON   Call a summon with the spirits on standby. See 5.5. Only shown
            when a summon is possible.
   ITEM     Use a Herb, Potion, Psy Drop or Elixir on an ally.
   DEFEND   Halves the damage taken this round.
   FLEE     60% chance to escape a random encounter. Impossible against
            bosses and spirit duels.

5.3 Arts (psynergy)
-------------------
Each character has three arts, learned at fixed party levels. Damage arts do
power + half your ATK + party level + 0 to 3, minus the enemy's DEF, and they
ignore the "shrugs off steel" reduction entirely. This is why arts, not
swords, kill spirits and bosses.

   Art          Who    Lv   PP   Power   Notes
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------
   QUAKE        Ari     1    4    18     earth, one enemy
   SPIRE        Ari     4    7    36     earth, one enemy
   GAIA BLADE   Ari     8   14    64     earth, one enemy
   FLARE        Lyn     1    4    20     fire, one enemy
   FIRE BOMB    Lyn     4    8    22     fire, ALL enemies
   INFERNO      Lyn     8   15    40     fire, ALL enemies
   CURE         Bram    1    4    -      heals 70 HP, one ally
   ICE HORN     Bram    4    6    34     water, one enemy
   PURE PLY     Bram    7   10    -      fully heals one ally
   GUST         Sera    1    3    16     wind, one enemy
   PLASMA       Sera    5    9    28     wind, ALL enemies
   BOLT         Sera    8   12    58     wind, one enemy

PP comes back slowly as you walk: every member regains 1 PP per 8 steps.
Psy Drops restore 30 PP, inns, the Thera well and the spirit spring restore
all of it, and so does a level-up.

5.4 Spirits: set, unleash, standby
----------------------------------
There are eight spirits, two per element. Walking onto one on the map starts
a DUEL: a battle against the spirit's battle form that you cannot flee. Win
and it joins you; lose and the party collapses as usual (see 5.6) but the
spirit stays where it was for a rematch. Spirits take only 30% damage from
FIGHT, so use arts. The last four (Breeze, Ember, Mist, Squall) act twice
every round.

Every spirit you own starts each battle SET. A set spirit gives EVERY party
member +1 ATK and +1 DEF, so eight set spirits are +8/+8 for the whole team.
Set spirits are also what you unleash:

   SPIRIT command -> the spirit does its effect and goes to STANDBY.
   Standby spirits no longer give their +1/+1 (your ATK and DEF drop by
   one each for the rest of the fight), but they can power a SUMMON.
   After a summon the spirits it used are SPENT for the rest of the battle.

Everything resets at the end of each battle: all spirits are set again next
time. There is no recovery timer and no need to manage them between fights.
In short: unleash freely in boss fights, summon with what is on standby.

Unleash damage is spirit power + 4 x party level + 0 to 3, minus HALF the
enemy's DEF. See [SPR08] for the eight effects.

5.5 Summons
-----------
A summon needs a number of STANDBY spirits of its element (or one of each of
the four elements for the Sol Avatar). It hits every enemy for power + 5 x
party level + 0 to 5, minus half their DEF, then the spirits used are spent.
The summon plays a cutscene. Full list in [SUM09].

Because the spirits must be on standby first, a summon always costs at least
one earlier turn of unleashing. The classic rhythm against a boss is: round
one, everyone unleashes; round two, someone summons while the others cast
arts.

5.6 Random encounters, fleeing and defeat
-----------------------------------------
Battles only happen on TALL GRASS (fields and trail) and ORE-flecked floor
(mine and spire). Every step on such a tile, once at least 5 steps have
passed since the last battle, has a 15% chance of a fight. Plain ground,
paths and cliff tops are always safe, so you can cross most of a map without
a single fight if you stick to them. Which groups appear is in [BST13].

Thera Fields only sends lone monsters (or a pair of oozes) while the party
is below level 3.

If every member falls, the party wakes up in Thera at the starting spot with
full HP and PP. You lose NOTHING: no coins, no items, no progress. Defeat is
just a free trip home, so there is little reason to ever load a save.

Members knocked out during a battle you still win get back up with 1 HP
afterwards.

5.7 Saving
----------
   - SAVE in the pause menu, any time outside battle.
   - The game also saves by itself whenever you change maps, win a battle,
     recruit a spirit or sleep at an inn.
   - One save slot, kept in the browser (localStorage). CONTINUE on the
     title screen loads it. Clearing site data deletes it.

5.8 Shops, inns, wells and the spring
-------------------------------------
   Thera shop     The third house on the lane (the door at (21,19)).
                  Mara's goods: basics and early gear. [SHP12]
   Brindle shop   There is no shop building. Talk to DOV on the west side
                  of the plaza; he sells the good stuff. [SHP12]
   Inns           Mira Farmstead (east of Thera) and Brindle. 12 coins
                  restores everyone, revives the fallen and saves.
   Thera well     The well at (15,22), south-west of the signpost. Walk
                  onto it: full HP and PP for everyone, free, any time.
   Spirit spring  In the Sunspire at (3,17). Same effect, free. This is
                  your base camp for the final boss.

5.9 The damage formulas
-----------------------
For the curious (rnd(a,b) is a random whole number from a to b):

   FIGHT      max(1, ATK + rnd(0,4) - enemy DEF), then x0.3 vs spirits,
              x0.5 vs the Ogre, x0.45 vs the Wyrm
   ART        max(1, power + ATK/2 + level + rnd(0,3) - enemy DEF)
   UNLEASH    max(1, power + 4*level + rnd(0,3) - enemy DEF/2)
   SUMMON     max(1, power + 5*level + rnd(0,5) - enemy DEF/2), all foes
   ENEMY HIT  max(1, enemy ATK + rnd(0,4) - your DEF); halved if defending
   ENEMY AOE  as above with enemy ATK x0.7 (Ogre SMASH) or x0.75 (Wyrm's
              SHADOW FLAME), on everyone

Your ATK = base + weapon + number of SET spirits.
Your DEF = base + armour + number of SET spirits.

The game opens with Sorin's briefing: the Sunspire is dark, eight spirits
fled, FLINT hides west of the houses, Lyn went north to Brindle and is stuck
behind a boulder. Ari stands on the trail below the village with 30 coins,
a Wood Blade and three Herbs.

First, some shopping. Walk north up the trail two tiles and turn left along
the village lane. SORIN stands by the lane (11,21) and repeats the briefing
with one extra clue: a second spirit "skims the lake". MARA wanders near
(16,20) and tells you her door is open. POLLY, the child at (24,21), saw a
stone critter behind the west house - that is Flint.

The SHOP is the third house, the one right of the trail: stand at (21,20)
facing up at the door (21,19) and press A. With 30 coins the best buy is the
TRAVEL VEST (18 coins, DEF+2): Ari's defence is the one stat that matters in
the first fights. Keep the remaining 12 coins for a Herb later, or just hold
on to them. (The Bronze Sword at 80 is the next upgrade; you will have the
coins for it after a few fights, and there is a free one in the mine.)

Read the SIGNPOST at (20,20) if you like: north is the fence gate and
Brindle, west is Lake Mira, east is Mira Farmstead and its inn.

Now the well. It is the "+" at (15,22), just south-west of the signpost.
Walking onto it fully heals Ari whenever anyone is hurt. Remember it: every
Thera-area fight below can be followed by a free heal, which makes early
levelling painless.

LEVELLING UP A LITTLE
Before Flint, get Ari to level 2 (30 XP) and ideally level 3 (72 XP). Walk
the tall grass between the lane and the lake. Below level 3 the fields only
send single monsters or an ooze pair, and Ari with the Travel Vest handles
every one of them with FIGHT alone:

   Mire Ooze   22 HP   8 XP   7 coins   may drop a Herb (20%)
   Fang Wolf   24 HP  14 XP  12 coins   may drop a Herb (18%)
   Dusk Bat    18 HP  10 XP   8 coins   drains a third of its damage back
   Pit Spider  28 HP  13 XP  11 coins

Use QUAKE (4 PP, ~20 damage) when something is still standing after a hit,
heal at the well when low, and open the pause menu's SAVE now and then.
Chest 1, the 25 coins at (32,11), sits in the grass band north of the
lakeside clearing: from the lane go up the trail to row 12, east along the
clearing to (32,12), face up, press A.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7.2 FLINT AND THE BOULDER                                            [WLK7B]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended level: 2 to 3

Go to the far west end of the lane. Flint sits at (3,20), the "*" left of the
first house. Step onto it: the duel starts and you cannot run.

   SPIRIT DUEL: FLINT (earth)
   HP 70   ATK 14   DEF 5   AGI 10   Reward: 24 XP, 12 coins, FLINT
   Takes 30% from FIGHT. Acts once per round.

   Flint hits Ari for about 9 to 13 a round without the vest, a few less
   with it. QUAKE does 20 to 23 per cast here, so three casts and one or
   two swings finish it. At level 1 Ari only has 12 PP, which is exactly
   three Quakes: cast all three, then FIGHT for the last few points, and
   eat a Herb if HP falls under 15. At level 2 or 3 it is routine.

Flint joins (1/8) and the game tells you its power can shatter boulders. Walk
back to the well to heal, then go up the trail to the boulder at (18,8) and
walk into it. FLINT cracks it and the way north opens. The QUEST line now
says "FIND LYN'S COMPASS IN THE NORTH PADDOCK".

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7.3 THE PADDOCK AND LYN'S COMPASS                                    [WLK7C]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

North of the fence lies the paddock, rows 2 to 7 of the map. The trail runs
straight up its middle to the exit at (18,0), but you want the far north-west
corner first. Row 4 has been cleared of trees for you: from the trail step
onto (17,4) and walk west along row 4 to (3,4). Chest 2 is the "2" at (3,3)
directly above you: face up, press A, and you have LYN'S COMPASS (it appears
under ITEMS as a KEY item). The paddock grass has the same monsters as the
rest of the fields.

Back to the trail and north through (18,0) to Brindle. You can also come back
for DEW now (7.6), but it is a harder duel than Flint and easier with two
members, so this guide fetches Lyn first.

  7.4 MIRA FARMSTEAD (OPTIONAL)                                        [WLK7D]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The road east from the lane (row 21, x 34 to 44) then south down x=44 leads
to Mira Farmstead, an open meadow with two homes, an inn and HOB the farmer.
Nothing here is required, but it is worth one trip:

   - Chest 3 at (50,36), by the east hedge: a POTION (220 HP). Stand at
     (50,37) facing up, or at (49,36) facing right.
   - The INN (door at (44,34), stand at (44,35) facing up): 12 coins for a
     full heal and a save. Since the Thera well is free, you will only
     ever use it to revive a fallen member before the mine.
   - Hob warns about the tall grass; the meadow's monsters are the same
     Thera Fields set, and the meadow is a good place to grind to level 3
     or 4 if the mine is giving you trouble later.

  7.6 LAKE MIRA: DEW                                                   [WLK7F]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended level: 3 to 4, with Lyn

With Lyn aboard, go back south to Thera Fields for the second spirit. DEW
sits on the east shore of Lake Mira at (8,12), in the lakeside clearing (row

   SPIRIT DUEL: DEW (water)
   HP 120   ATK 20   DEF 6   AGI 12   Reward: 30 XP, 14 coins, DEW

free emergency heal in every fight from here on. Heal at the well, save,

You CAN do this duel with Ari alone at level 3 (QUAKE four times plus Flint
is just enough), but the margin is thin.

The layout: four north-south shafts separated by rock walls at x=7, x=14
and x=21, joined only by the wide main gallery along rows 10 and 11 (the

monsters are.

The moment you step in, BRAM blocks the tunnel, grins, and joins (3/4). He
arrives with CURE, a lot of HP and nothing equipped, so sort out EQUIP right
away. He wants the best armour you own.

The mine's monsters are a big step up from the fields:

   Ore Beetle   70 HP   ATK 24   22 XP   16 coins   may drop a Travel Vest
   Ruin Golem   95 HP   ATK 28   30 XP   22 coins   may drop a Psy Drop

Two golems or a golem and a beetle in one fight will chew through 40 HP in
a round. Use arts on the big ones (Golem DEF 11 blunts FIGHT badly), keep
Bram on CURE duty, and use DEW's heal before anyone drops. If it goes wrong,
defeat only costs you the walk back from Thera.

Suggested route (fight on the ore as you go; you want the XP):

1. GRANITE is right there, the "*" at (3,18), two tiles north-east of where
   you stand. You can take it immediately or come back when you are a level
   higher - it is tougher than Dew.

   SPIRIT DUEL: GRANITE (earth)
   HP 220   ATK 34   DEF 11   AGI 8   Reward: 40 XP, 24 coins, GRANITE
   Takes 30% from FIGHT. Acts once per round. Slow: everyone outruns it.

   Round one: Flint unleash (about 45) plus QUAKE and FLARE (about 25
   each, less with its DEF 11). Dew's heal when someone passes half HP.
   Bram casts CURE or ICE HORN (level 4). Five rounds or so. Granite
   hits for 20 to 30 against light armour: never let anyone sit below 30.

2. Go up the west shaft (x 1 to 6) to chest 1 at (4,3): the BRONZE SWORD.
   Equip it on Ari at once (ATK+7) and pass the Wood Blade to Lyn.

3. The wall at x=7 stops short of the top three rows, so from the chest you
   can walk straight east along row 3 into the second shaft (x 8 to 13).
   FORGE is the "*" at (12,3), at the end of that row.

   SPIRIT DUEL: FORGE (fire)
   HP 200   ATK 35   DEF 10   AGI 11   Reward: 40 XP, 24 coins, FORGE
   Takes 30% from FIGHT. Acts once per round.

   Same plan as Granite; a touch less HP. With Granite in hand you now
   have two earth spirits on standby after unleashing both, which means
   TITAN (110 power) is available in round two - it alone takes a third
   of Forge's HP.

4. Chest 2 at (13,12), the PSY DROP, hangs just below the gallery on the
   second shaft's side: from (13,11) face down and press A.

5. Cross to the east shaft (x 22 to 26) and go down to chest 3 at (24,18)
   for 60 coins. You are now carrying 85 coins from chests alone plus the
   duel and monster money: pop back to Brindle (the exit is at the far
   south-west) for CHAIN MAIL and the inn if you need to.

6. The MAW OGRE sits at (25,10), right in front of the east exit. Make sure
   everyone is at full HP and PP (the inn, or walk the safe gallery until PP
   ticks back), save, and step into it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7.8 BOSS: MAW OGRE                                                   [BOS7H]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   MAW OGRE
   HP 650   ATK 42   DEF 10   AGI 6
   FIGHT does only 50% damage. Cannot flee.
   Attacks: a single strike for ATK 42 + 0-4 minus your DEF, or (35% of
   its turns) a SMASH on the whole party at 70% strength.
   Reward: 150 XP, 200 coins, THE SPIRE KEY

   Recommended: party level 6, four spirits (Flint, Dew, Granite, Forge),
   Chain Mail on Bram, Leather Mail on the others.

With AGI 6 the Ogre is the slowest thing in the game: your whole party acts
before it every round, which turns the fight into arithmetic.

   Round 1   Ari: unleash FLINT.    Lyn: unleash FORGE.
             Bram: unleash GRANITE.
             Three unleashes land about 60 each at level 6 - the Ogre is
             already down a quarter. Its first hit lands.
   Round 2   Ari: SUMMON TITAN (two earth spirits on standby, ~135).
             Lyn: SUMMON SALAMAND (one fire, ~80) or FLARE.
             Bram: CURE whoever was hit, or ICE HORN if nobody needs it.
             The Ogre is now around 300 HP.
   Round 3+  Arts every turn: SPIRE from Ari (~45), FLARE (~30) or FIRE
             BOMB from Lyn, ICE HORN (~40) from Bram. Bram switches to CURE
             the moment anyone is under 30 HP. Use DEW's heal (55% of max)
             as the big emergency button; it is faster than a Potion.

A single Ogre hit takes 20 to 26 HP off a Chain Mail wearer and 25 to 32 off
the others; SMASH does about 10 to 20 to everyone at once. Lyn at level 6
has 49 HP, so two strikes in a row on her will drop her. If she falls, do
not panic: finish the fight and she gets up with 1 HP, or spend an Elixir
only if Bram cannot keep up. Expect the fight to run five or six rounds.

Victory gives the SPIRE KEY and a fat purse. The mine's east exit at (26,10)
is open: walk through it to the Sunspire Trail. Before you do, consider a
last trip to Brindle: the 200 coins from the Ogre buy an IRON AXE for Ari or
a second CHAIN MAIL, and both make the trail much kinder.

   ICE HORN. Dew's heal and Potions whenever anyone is under 35. If it
   goes badly, retreat is impossible, but a wipe only sends you to Thera
   with everything intact - walk back and try again a level higher.

   FIGHT does only 45% damage. Cannot flee.
   Attacks: a single strike at ATK 56 + 0-4 minus your DEF, or (40% of
   its turns) SHADOW FLAME on the whole party at 75% strength.
   Special: while below 55% HP it may spend a turn to HEAL 220 HP
   (50% chance each turn it is eligible), up to TWICE per battle.
   Effective HP: up to 1640.
   Reward: the ending.

   Recommended: party level 10, all eight spirits, Sol Blade on Ari,
   Steel Rapier and Iron Axe on two others, Chain Mail or better on
   everyone, three Potions and two Elixirs.

The Wyrm is slow (AGI 9): at level 10 the whole party acts before it every
round, Sera first. It is the only enemy that heals, and its heal is the
whole fight: 1200 HP plus two 220-point heals is 1640 HP to burn through,
and each heal is also a turn it does not attack. The trick is to bring so
much damage in the first three rounds that it is dead before the second
heal matters.

   Round 1   Everyone UNLEASHES: Ari FLINT, Lyn FORGE, Bram GRANITE,
             Sera BREEZE (stagger: the Wyrm loses its turn!).
             Unleashes at level 10 do 60 to 95 each - about 280 damage,
             and the Wyrm has not moved.
   Round 2   Four spirits on standby, one of each element: Ari SUMMONS
             SOL AVATAR (200 power, about 245 damage at level 10). Lyn
             unleashes EMBER (~90), Sera unleashes SQUALL (~55), Bram
             unleashes MIST (heals everyone 35%) or casts CURE as needed.
             The Wyrm is down to about 550.
   Round 3   Sol Avatar spent one spirit of each element, so the two-
             spirit summons are off the table, but the singles are not:
             Lyn SUMMONS SALAMAND (Ember), Sera SUMMONS SYLPH (Squall -
             it staggers again!), Bram SUMMONS NIXIE (Mist: damage plus a
             30% heal for everyone) or casts CURE, Ari casts GAIA BLADE
             (~100). The Wyrm drops under 55% here and starts rolling
             for heals.
   Round 4+  Arts: GAIA BLADE, INFERNO, BOLT, ICE HORN. Bram on PURE PLY
             (full heal, 10 PP) the moment anyone is under 40. Potions
             from Sera, who acts first, if two people are low.

An alternative opening that many players prefer: unleash Flint and Granite
in round one with Ari and Bram while Lyn and Sera cast, then TITAN in round
two (two earth, 110 power), and build Sol Avatar in rounds two and three
instead. Both work; the Sol Avatar-first line front-loads the most damage.

Damage you take: a single strike is 30 to 40 through Chain Mail, 25 to 35
through Aegis Plate (a few more once your spirits are off standby, since
the set bonus goes with them); SHADOW FLAME is about 20 to 30 on everyone.
At level
10 Lyn has 69 HP and Sera 71, so two strikes in a row on either is a
knock-out. Keep them above 45 and use the Elixirs without hesitation -
there is nothing to save them for.

When it falls, the Wyrm dissolves into mist and warm light floods the
tower.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7.12 THE ENDING                                                      [END7L]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ending screen shows the sun rising over the spire with your party level,
spirits found, battles won and coins. Press Enter or A after a moment to
return to a fresh game. The victory is saved: CONTINUE on the title screen
drops you in the Sunspire with the Wyrm gone and Sorin, back in Thera, has a
new line for you. There is no post-game content beyond that; the chests you
missed can still be opened.

Unleash effects (power + 4 x level + 0-3, minus half the target's DEF):

   FLINT     32 earth damage to one enemy. Also shatters the Thera boulder.
   GRANITE   48 earth damage to one enemy.
   FORGE     40 fire damage to one enemy.
   EMBER     56 fire damage to one enemy. The strongest single unleash.
   DEW       Heals one ally for 55% of their max HP.
   MIST      Heals the whole party for 35% of each member's max HP.
   BREEZE    24 wind damage and STAGGERS the target: it loses its next
             action (one strike of a double-striker's two, or the Wyrm's
             whole turn). Breeze is the best spirit in the game.
   SQUALL    22 wind damage to EVERY enemy.

Set bonus: +1 ATK and +1 DEF to every member per set spirit (max +8/+8).
All spirits are set again at the start of every battle.

Order of recruitment in this guide: Flint, Dew, Granite, Forge, Ember,
Breeze, Mist, Squall. Only Flint is required to finish the game (for the
boulder); the rest are optional but you will not beat the Wyrm without most
of them.

  9. SUMMON LIST                                                       [SUM09]
===============================================================================

A summon needs the listed spirits ON STANDBY (unleashed earlier in the same
battle). It hits every enemy for power + 5 x level + 0-5, minus half their
DEF, then spends those spirits. The SUMMON icon only appears when at least
one summon is possible.

   Summon       Element  Needs                  Power  Extra
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   GNOME        earth    1 earth on standby       50
   TITAN        earth    2 earth on standby      110
   SALAMAND     fire     1 fire on standby        56
   PHOENIX      fire     2 fire on standby       120
   NIXIE        water    1 water on standby       40   heals party 30%
   LEVIATH      water    2 water on standby      104
   SYLPH        wind     1 wind on standby        46   staggers all foes
   TEMPEST      wind     2 wind on standby       112
   SOL AVATAR   all      1 of EACH element       200   needs all four
                         on standby                    elements found

Rules of thumb
   - A two-spirit summon is always better than two one-spirit summons
     (Titan 110 vs Gnome 50 twice), but it needs two of the same element,
     so it costs two unleash turns.
   - Sol Avatar uses one spirit of each element and leaves the other four
     for a second wave: Titan/Phoenix/Leviath/Tempest are impossible after
     it (only one of each element is left), but Gnome, Salamand, Nixie and
     Sylph still are.
   - Sylph's stagger on every enemy is the best "oh no" button in a
     two-gargoyle fight.

  14. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS                                       [FAQ14]
===============================================================================

Q: I can't get past the boulder north of Thera.
A: You need FLINT, the earth spirit at (3,20) west of the houses. Beat it in
   its duel, then walk into the boulder.

Q: Lyn won't join. She keeps talking about a compass.
A: It is in the chest at (3,3), the north-west corner of the paddock behind
   the boulder. Bring it back and say YES.

Q: Where is the shop in Brindle?
A: There is no building. Talk to DOV, the man standing on the west side of
   the plaza at (8,9).

Q: My attacks do 2 damage to a spirit / the boss!
A: That is intended. Spirits take 30% of FIGHT damage and bosses about
   half. Use ARTS, SPIRIT unleashes and SUMMONS - they are not reduced.

Q: The SUMMON icon never appears.
A: Summons need spirits ON STANDBY, which means you have to UNLEASH them
   with the SPIRIT command first in the same battle. Unleash one earth
   spirit, and next round Gnome is available.

Q: Why did my ATK go down mid-battle?
A: Each SET spirit gives +1 ATK and +1 DEF. Unleashing moves it to standby
   and the bonus goes with it until the battle ends.

Q: I lost a spirit duel. Is the spirit gone?
A: No. It stays on the map for a rematch, and you lose nothing on defeat.

Q: How do I unlock the gate on the Sunspire Trail?
A: Beat the MAW OGRE in Brindle Mine; he drops the SPIRE KEY. Then walk
   into the gate.

Q: I'm out of PP in the middle of the mine.
A: PP comes back at 1 per 8 steps for everyone. Pace the safe gallery for a
   while, use a Psy Drop, or go back to the Brindle inn (12 coins).

Q: Is there a free heal anywhere?
A: Yes, two: the well at (15,22) in Thera and the spirit spring at (3,17)
   in the Sunspire. Walk onto them.

Q: Is there anything missable?
A: No. Every chest and spirit can be collected at any point, including
   after the ending.

Q: Can I sell gear?
A: No. There is no selling. Spend carefully.

Q: Where is my save?
A: In the browser's local storage for the site you played on. Play from the
   same browser and address to continue. Private browsing or clearing site
   data wipes it.

Q: What are sprite packs?
A: A way to replace every sprite, tile and background with your own art.
   It is purely cosmetic and does not change any number in this guide.
   See the game's pack guide page.
