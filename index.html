The story

The Sunspire has gone dark

When the Shadow Wyrm coiled around the great tower, the eight elemental spirits that kept its light burning fled across the land, and monsters began to prowl every road.

In the quiet village of Thera, Elder Sorin turns to Ari, a young earth adept. Find the spirits. Gather allies. Climb the spire, and bring back the dawn.

The road runs north through Thera Fields to the mining town of Brindle, down into a mine where something enormous sits on the Spire Key, and up a harpy-haunted trail to the tower itself. Spirits fight before they join you; set them for strength, unleash them in battle, or spend them together on a summon.

Lyn Water

A sharp-tongued scout holed up in Brindle, the party's healer, and not walking anywhere without her compass.

Bram Fire

A broad miner who knows every shaft, and throws fire about as hard as he swings a pick.

Sera Wind

A scout pinned down by harpies on the high trail. Fast, precise, and done waiting.
