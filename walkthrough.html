===============================================================================
  1. INTRODUCTION                                                       [INT01]
===============================================================================

Sunspire Saga is an original homage to the handheld JRPGs of the early 2000s: a
party of four elemental adepts, eight collectible spirits that boost your stats
when set and power great summons when unleashed, turn-based battles, towns,
shops, inns, treasure chests and a dark tower at the end of the road.

This is the engine build of the game (sunspire-saga 0.0.1, game data V48): the
same story, maps, numbers and art as the single-script game, running on the
goldensun_html5 engine. It plays in the browser, saves there, and the handheld
shell around the screen works with touch.

How to use this guide: section 7 is the walkthrough in the order the game
expects, with a map, the chests and the enemies of every area. Sections 8 to 13
are reference lists. Map coordinates are (x,y): x counts tiles from the left
edge and y from the top, both from 0; the rulers on the maps show them.

This file is generated from the game's own data by
scripts/build_walkthrough.js, so every number and position in it is the one the
game uses.

===============================================================================
  3. STORY                                                              [STO03]
===============================================================================

The Sunspire, the great tower north of the village of Thera, has gone dark.
With its light gone, monsters prowl every road and the folk of Thera and the
mining town of Brindle have bolted their doors. The eight elemental spirits
that once lived in the spire scattered across the land when the SHADOW WYRM
took the top of the tower.

Ari, a young earth adept from Thera, is sent out by the elder Sorin to gather
the spirits and bring the dawn back. Along the way Ari is joined by Lyn, a
water adept scouting ahead in Brindle; Bram, a fire adept and miner who knows
every shaft of Brindle Mine; and Sera, a wind adept pinned down by harpies on
the Sunspire Trail.

  4. CONTROLS AND THE MENU                                              [CTL04]
===============================================================================

Keyboard
--------
   Arrow keys   Move; move the cursor in menus
   Z            A button: confirm, talk, open chests, read signs, open the menu
   X            B button: cancel, back, close
   A / S        L / R shoulder buttons: switch character or target
   Enter        Start: the save prompt; also confirms on the title screen
   Backspace    Select
   M            Toggle sound
   F            Immersive (full screen) mode

Touch
-----
The handheld shell around the screen has a D-pad, A, B, Select, Start and the L
/ R shoulders; the sound button beside the shell is the only way to unmute on a
phone. The shell colour and the light / dark theme are remembered by the
browser.

Interacting
-----------
Face a thing and press A: villagers, signs, doors, chests, the boulder and the
spire gate all respond. Spirits also respond to A: talk to one and the duel
starts. Bosses start their fight the moment you talk to them. With nothing in
front of you, A opens the main menu (items, psynergy, spirits, status).

Saving
------
Press Start (Enter) and confirm to save; the save lives in this browser. The
inns also save. If the party falls, you wake up in the last town you visited
with everyone alive.

  5. GAME BASICS                                                        [BAS05]
===============================================================================

5.1 Levels
----------
Each character levels on their own, with the experience from every battle they
survive. A new member joins at the level the story expects by then: Lyn at 3,
Bram at 5, Sera at 8. Experience needed for the next level is 18 + 12 x
(current level):

   Level  XP for next  Total XP from level 1
   -----  -----------  ---------------------
   1      30           0
   2      42           30
   3      54           72
   4      66           126
   5      78           192
   6      90           270
   7      102          360
   8      114          462
   9      126          576
   10     138          702
   11     150          840
   12     162          990

5.2 Battle
----------
Turns go by agility. A plain attack does (attack - the target's defence) / 2,
so steel does little to anything with a hard hide: the bosses and the spirits
shrug it off, and arts (psynergy) are the answer. An art does its listed power
as damage whatever the defence; area arts hit every foe. Defend halves the
damage taken that turn. Fleeing from a normal battle usually works; spirits and
bosses block every path.

5.3 Spirits
-----------
A spirit joins you after you beat it in a duel. SET, a spirit adds to the stats
of the character who holds it (hp +4, pp +2, attack +1, defence +1). In battle
you can UNLEASH a set spirit for its own effect (see section 8); it then stands
by, and standby spirits are what the great summons spend (section 9). After a
summon the spirits set themselves again over the next turns.

5.4 Items and gear
------------------
Herbs, potions, psy drops and elixirs are used from the item menu or in battle.
Weapons add attack and armour adds defence; equip them from the main menu.
Every character can wear everything.

5.5 Encounters
--------------
Random battles happen while you walk in the fields, the mine, the trail and the
spire; towns are safe. Each area has its own set of enemy groups (section 13
lists who lives where); the paddock behind the boulder in Thera Fields sends
packs at any level.

5.6 Field arts
--------------
Ari knows SHOVE from the start: press Q (the first psynergy shortcut) facing a
loose boulder to push it one tile for 2 PP, or pick it from the psynergy menu.
Walking into a loose boulder pushes it too. Boulders only move onto the tiles
the ground allows.

5.7 Sprite packs
----------------
Every sheet the game draws can be replaced with your own art from the play page
(Load sprite pack); the pack guide page lists every slot with its size and
frames.

Thera is the row of houses in the middle of the fields; you start by the well
(18,22). Talk to SORIN (11,21): he points you west. The shop is the house at
(20,17): its door at (21,19) opens Mara's counter. The well at (15,22) restores
the party.

FLINT waits at (3,20), left of the houses. Talk to it to duel it: it shrugs off
steel, so cast QUAKE every turn (3 casts at level 1, so an herb or a level
helps). With Flint set, the boulder at (18,8) on the trail north crumbles when
you talk to it.

Behind the boulder is the paddock: packs roam here at any level. LYN'S COMPASS
is in chest 3 at (3,3). DEW, the water spirit, skims the lake at (8,12) west of
the fence. The road north at (18,0) leads to Brindle.

East along the lane is Mira Farmstead: two homes, the inn (door at (44,34), 12
coins, saves the game), HOB by the hedge and a chest at (50,36).

   Chests:

  7.7. BOSS: SHADOW WYRM                                                [BOS7G]
===============================================================================

   HP           1200
   Attack       56
   Defence      high: steel does little
   XP / coins   0 / 0

The wyrm shrugs off steel, breathes SHADOW FLAME on the whole party and knits
its wounds twice when it is low. Rest at the spring first, set every spirit,
open with the strongest summons, then arts every turn; Lyn and the potions keep
the party standing.

===============================================================================
  7.8. THE ENDING                                                       [END7H]
===============================================================================

With the wyrm gone, warm light floods the Sunspire and the party returns to
Thera. Sorin has a last word for you, and the game can be played on from the
save.

  14. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS                                        [FAQ14]
===============================================================================

Q: Steel does nothing to the spirits and bosses. Is that a bug?
---------------------------------------------------------------
A: No. Their defence is far above your attack; cast arts and unleash spirits
instead.

Q: Where is Lyn's compass?
--------------------------
A: In the paddock north of the fence in Thera Fields, chest 3 at (3,3). You
need Flint to break the boulder first.

Q: The gate on the trail will not open.
---------------------------------------
A: The SPIRE KEY is what the Maw Ogre in Brindle Mine sits on.

Q: Is this the same game as the single-script version?
------------------------------------------------------
A: Same story, maps, numbers and art. The engine build levels characters one by
one and counts damage a little differently; this guide describes the engine
build.
