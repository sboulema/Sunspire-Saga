Sprite pack guide

Every sheet the game draws can be replaced with your own art. A pack is a set of image files, each named after the slot it replaces, loaded with Load sprite pack on the play page, one by one or as a zip. Packs stay in your own browser; nothing is uploaded.

Rules

  1. One file per slot, named exactly <slot>.png (.gif and .webp work too; only the first frame of a GIF is used). Folder names inside a zip are ignored.
  2. The image must have the exact size of the sheet it replaces, listed below, because the frame layout stays the same: the game cuts your sheet into the same frames at the same positions. Open the original sheet to see where each frame sits.
  3. Partial packs are fine; every slot you leave out keeps the built-in art. A file that does not match a slot, or has the wrong size, is reported and skipped.
  4. A loaded pack takes effect at once: the play page reloads and confirms how many sheets it replaced. Built-in art on the play page clears the pack.
  5. Only art you made yourself or have the rights to use. No sprites ripped from commercial games, in packs you share or in this repository.
